# Shoot moments

[中文](README.md) · **English**

> You remember a scene → it tells you the episode and the timestamp.

A scene index for Root and Shaw in Person of Interest. You remember a scene but not
which episode it was in. This gives you the episode, the start and end time, and the
dialogue from that scene.

**<https://shoot5313.github.io/shoot-moments/>**

## What's in it

136 entries, 1,058 lines of dialogue, 148 minutes in total, spread across 51 episodes.
Every entry was checked line by line against the subtitles.

Three threads you can look at separately: Root × Shaw, Root on her own, Shaw on her own.
Search it if you remember half a line. Browse by scene if you don't remember any words at all.

## Which release the timestamps follow

All timestamps follow the `HDTV.x264-LOL` subtitles. Other releases are usually off by a
constant offset, so line up one scene and the rest will follow.
